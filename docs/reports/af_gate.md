**Where AF fails (post2023, GNINA-CNN).** AFDB success drops with pocket side-chain RMSD: 0.22 (≤1 Å, n=45), 0.16 (1–2 Å, n=63), 0.06 (>2 Å, n=17). Holo success in the same bins: 0.49 / 0.43 / 0.35. Even near-holo AF pockets lose about half their successes. Metal sites: AFDB 0.10 vs 0.19 without metals; AF receptors carry no ions or cofactors. AFDB v4 vs v6: 0.14 vs 0.20 (n=69/56; difference not tested).
**Arms (Uni-Dock backend, AFDB receptor, post2023 n=125).**
- A2: ligand-aware side-chain relaxation (GNINA Vina-like minimisation, flexdist 3.5 Å around all 9 poses, rerank). Round 1: 0.17 (no change). Round 2 (splice the best side chains, re-dock, relax): 0.16. Median time 6.4 s / 19.3 s vs 3.8 s for Uni-Dock.
- A1: soft receptor (pocket side chains truncated to Ala), docked, then relaxed in the full AFDB receptor. Results are paired differences vs AFDB Uni-Dock (0.17).
  - All pocket side chains: 0.00 (−17 [−24,−10]).
  - Long side chains only (R/K/E/Q/M): 0.06 (−11). After relaxation: 0.11 (−6 [−12,0]).
  - Only side chains with pLDDT<70: 0.18 (+2 [−2,+6]). After relaxation: 0.18 (+1). In most pockets nothing was truncated under this rule.
  - Conclusion: truncating side chains removes the pocket shape the scoring function needs; A1 as implemented is negative. val_astex (n=20) shows the same pattern.
**Timing** (node load average 25–55 on 16 cores from other users, so wall times are noisy). Median per-complex docking: Uni-Dock 3.8 s, GNINA no-CNN 6.2 s, GNINA-CNN 10.8 s (4 CPU + GPU). Boltz-2 amortised over a batch: ss 5.2 s per complex (123 in 652 s); MSA 48 s per complex (ColabFold server-bound, 99 min for 123, 4 failed).
**Conclusions.** (1) On the Boltz-safe set the AF gap is large and real: about −27 points for the C1 baseline. (2) Ligand-free single-sequence Boltz-2 is useless on novel proteins (wrong folds); adding the ligand does not rescue it. Boltz-2 MSA pockets match AFDB accuracy but dock no better. Co-folding arms (A4) should be deprioritised unless they use MSAs and show a gain on val_af_post2023. (3) Physics-only local side-chain relaxation (A2) does not help (±0), and soft receptors (A1) hurt or are neutral. Pocket side-chain RMSD explains only part of the gap. Missing metals/cofactors and sub-Å pocket shifts matter, so cofactor/ion placement and ensemble/rescoring arms deserve priority.
**Unverified / caveats.** n=125 with wide CIs; seed 0 only; no PB-validity co-metric yet; the preregistered GNINA `--flexdist` flexible baseline was implemented (`gnina_flex` in af_dock.py) but not run on val. GNINA was not run on Boltz-ss receptors for post2023 (budget; those pockets are wrong folds). val_dockgen/val_plinder have Uni-Dock only. The A1 pocket residue set comes from the reference ligand, as in the DESIGN emulated pocket finder. GNINA thread count was 4 on the main runs and 3 on Boltz-MSA/A2 runs. The Uni-Dock spacing fallback (>0.375 Å) applied to boxes over 30 Å (recorded in the `error` column as a note). AFDB v6 template leakage is possible. The data agent regenerated the val_astex records at 17:19 and I re-ran everything for val_astex (a MANIFEST guard now prevents this). The holo success rate on post2023 (44%) is lower than typical PoseBusters numbers; this is plausible for novel PLINDER systems but I have not investigated it.
