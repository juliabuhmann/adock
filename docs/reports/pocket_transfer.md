# Pocket-conformation transfer (post-test follow-up, VAL ONLY). pocketxfer agent, 2026-10-27 09:58–10:35 CET (`date`). First attempt, not independently re-computed
**Idea.** Copy the pocket conformation of date-safe holo homolog templates onto the AF model without using the ligand, then dock with frozen v2_fitted. Templates may hold any ligand.
**Code.** `src/adock/pocketxfer/{search,transfer}.py`, `scripts/pocketxfer_{run,summarize,samelig}.py`, `scripts/pocketxfer_val.sh`, `tests/test_pocketxfer.py` (1/1: a holo self-copy gives pocket RMSD 0, chi transfer lowers side-chain RMSD and leaves the backbone fixed). No existing file was changed, so frozen presets are byte-identical. Template DB rebuilt with `afhybrid_setup.sh`; FASTA md5 2ae8dacc… is identical to before. Corrected receptor PDBs are in /tmp/adock/pocketxfer.
**Method.** Templates and leakage rules come from `adock.template` unchanged: MMseqs2, date strictly before the query, T1/T2/T3 incl. global identity, pocket Cα Kabsch onto the AF pocket. "Holo" = at least one non-artifact ligand of ≥ 6 heavy atoms in the pocket. Ranking: identical pocket residues, then identity. Arms per tier: `sc1` (χ of identical residues from the best template), `sc1c` (+ conservative substitutions), `bb1` (backbone + side-chain coordinates of identical residues), `cons` (medoid χ over the top 4), and ensembles `ens_p`/`ens_bb_p`: AF + up to 4 corrected receptors, choosing the lowest top-1 score, with a penalty P ∈ {0, 0.5, 1} added for corrected receptors (afmode logic). Light clash relief: revert a residue when it overlaps another residue at < 0.75 Σr. Oracles use the query's own holo pocket: `orc_sc` (holo χ), `orc_bbsc` (holo backbone + side chains), `orc_bb` (holo backbone only), `orc_scl` (holo side chains placed on the AF backbone). Protocol: select on post23-AF + PLINDER-AF val_select (n=109), seeds 0–1. The finalist was frozen in `results/pocketxfer/selection.json` (10:20) before val_confirm (n=107) was run once.

- Finalist on confirm, per set at T1: post23 +4.0 [0, 8.9], PLINDER +7.8 [1.1, 16.7]. The corrected receptor wins the score in only 15 % of complexes at T1, 8–16 % at T2 and 1–2 % at T3.
- **The gain mostly comes from same-ligand templates** (`pocketxfer_samelig.py`, both halves pooled). Where the top template carries the query's own CCD, Δ is +13.5 at T1 (n=37) and +11.8 at T2 (n=34). Otherwise Δ is +3.9 (n=114) and +1.6 (n=92). At T3 there is no gain either way.
- So it is mainly information transfer via the induced pocket, like the template-first hybrid, but much weaker than that hybrid's +21 / +15 / +5.
**Coverage** (≥ 1 date-safe holo template): T1 69–71 %, T2 50–66 %, T3 26–29 %. Median 3 holo templates per query.
**Pocket RMSD to holo** (covered complexes, medians): Templates do not move the pocket toward holo. Side-chain RMSD 1.1–1.3 → 1.0–1.3 Å; all-atom 0.8–1.0 → 0.8–1.0 Å, for every arm and tier. Same-protein holo templates (fident ≥ 0.95, T1 bb1, select seed 0, n=34): all-atom 0.86 → 0.85 Å; closer than AF in 59 % of cases (26 % for lower identity). Holo pockets bound to other ligands differ from the query's holo pocket about as much as AF does. Oracles: χ transfer only reaches 0.7–0.8 Å side-chain RMSD (AF backbone and CB kept).
**Time per pocket** (6 CPU workers, shared node): holo-template search median 2.7 s [p90 5.5] on post23 and 1.5 s [3.4] on PLINDER, with MMseqs2 amortised. Transfer + clash relief is about 0.01 s per receptor. Docking is about 0.11 s per receptor in batch, so the 5-receptor ensemble costs about 0.5 s.
**Reading.** The ligand-independent part of the AF gap is in pocket geometry that includes the backbone: the oracle gains +12 to +22, versus 0 to +6 from χ alone. Holo homolog templates do not supply that geometry unless they hold the same ligand. The finalist gives a small, confirmed T1/T2 gain (+3 to +6) that is mostly same-ligand leakage of information. It is legitimate under the date rule but is not a pocket-conformation effect. T3 gives nothing. Not recommended over the template-first hybrid. Possibly worth stacking as the hybrid's ADock fallback, but that is untested.
**Not verified / caveats.** 13 arms × 3 tiers were selected on val_select, and the finalist is one of 3 penalties. Only seeds 0–1. CIs are complex-level, not UniProt-clustered. n ≈ 108 per half. PLINDER is pre-2023 (AFDB and template leakage are possible). GPU docking is not bit-reproducible: the AF control on select was 18.3 vs 17.9 % across two identical reruns (1 complex-seed). Only the second run is kept. Clash relief reverts many residues (median 3–6 per template); its threshold was not tuned. `bb1` creates peptide-bond discontinuities at copied residues; scoring ignores them. The pocket (box.json residues) comes from the crystal ligand, as everywhere in this project. Rows in `results/val_runs.tsv` are tagged `pocketxfer:*`. Tables are `results/pocketxfer/summary_val_{select,confirm}.csv`; per-complex CSVs are in the same directory.
